About this image converter

Drop in a photo and pick a target format. Converting to JPEG fills any transparent areas with white first, since JPEG has no alpha channel — everything else (PNG, WebP) preserves transparency. For JPEG and WebP output you also get a quality slider, since both are lossy formats.

How to convert an image

  1. Drop, browse to, or paste an image into the box above.
  2. Choose the format you want to convert to.
  3. Adjust quality if converting to JPEG or WebP.
  4. Click Download.

FAQ

Why did my transparent PNG get a white background?

That happens only when converting to JPEG, which can't store transparency at all. Convert to PNG or WebP instead if you need to keep a transparent background.

Which format is smallest?

For photos, WebP is usually the smallest at a given visual quality, followed by JPEG. PNG is best for graphics, logos, and screenshots with flat colors or text. See the format guide for details.

Can I convert and resize at the same time?

This tool only changes format. Use the resizer first (it also has a format option) if you want both in one pass.
